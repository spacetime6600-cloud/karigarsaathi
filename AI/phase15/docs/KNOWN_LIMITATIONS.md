# Phase 15 — Known Limitations and System Scope

## 1. Transparent Scope Boundaries
1. **Not a Trained ML Black-Box**: The Phase 15 Fair Price Assistant is explicitly rules-first. No claims of a trained statistical regression or deep learning pricing model are made unless empirical evaluation datasets are provided.
2. **No Automated Market Scraping**: The system does not silently scrape e-commerce websites or fabricate competitor listings. Market comparables must be authenticated with human verifier provenance.
3. **No Automatic Price Publishing**: The system provides cost estimates and suggestions; it never automatically updates live product listings in the marketplace without explicit artisan confirmation.
4. **Inflation & Macroeconomics**: Raw material costs and guild rates must be updated periodically by artisans or guild administrators to reflect localized price inflation.
